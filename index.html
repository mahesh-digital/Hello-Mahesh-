DailyBudget needs to be connected to Supabase

Open index.html in a text editor and find the two configuration constants near the top of the script:

const SUPABASE_URL = 'https://YOUR-PROJECT-REF.supabase.co';
const SUPABASE_KEY = 'YOUR-PUBLISHABLE-OR-ANON-KEY';

Replace them with the Project URL and the publishable (or legacy anon) key from Supabase → Project Settings → API. Never use the service-role key here.

Full step-by-step instructions are in README.md. Also run supabase_setup.sql once in the Supabase SQL Editor.

Privacy and security

  • Your account uses Supabase Authentication. Passwords are never stored by this app.
  • Every record is tied to your account. The database itself (Row Level Security) prevents other users from reading or changing your data.
  • You enter your numbers by hand. DailyBudget never asks for bank logins or card numbers and never connects to your bank.
  • Your transactions are not sent to any AI or analytics service. Insights are calculated in your browser.
  • You can export your data or permanently delete your account at any time from Settings.

This site is hosted publicly on GitHub Pages, so anyone can read the app's source code. That is normal and safe: your private data lives in the database behind your login, not in the code.

DailyBudget — for personal budgeting only. Not financial advice. Balances and savings are numbers you enter yourself and are not verified by any bank.
